# **Settings**

Settings is where an administrator configures Horilla HR for the company: organisation structure, access, formats, email, appearance and integrations. Open it with the gear icon in the top bar; it opens at **System Preferences**. Settings are **company-specific**: a banner at the top of every page reads *You are currently setting these settings for the Your Company company specifically.*

## **Weekly Off Days**

**Settings → Organization → Weekly Off Days** defines recurring company-wide days off based on the week of the month and the weekday. The table lists **Based On Week, Based On Week Day, Company** and **Actions**. The demo has three rules: **Fourth Week Saturday**, **Second Week Saturday** and **All Sunday** (the week shows as **All**, meaning every week). The page has a search box, **Filter**, **Create** and a bulk **Select** option.

## **Encashment Settings**

**Settings → Leave → Encashment Settings** has two parts:

- **Encashment Redeem Condition**: **Bonus Unit** (the amount credited for each bonus point redeemed) and **Leave Unit** (the amount credited for each leave unit redeemed), each with its own **Save**. The demo shows 25 and 1500.
- **Leave Encashment Eligibility**: the **Enable Leave Encashment** switch shows or hides the Leave Encashments section for employees. **Apply to all employees** is on in the demo; turning it off lets you choose by **Employees**, **Department** and **Job Position**.
