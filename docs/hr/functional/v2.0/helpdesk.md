# **Tickets (Helpdesk)**

The Tickets module is the internal helpdesk: employees raise tickets, they are routed to a person, position or department, and they are worked until resolved. It is named **Tickets** in the sidebar and **Helpdesk** on its pages. Open it from **Tickets**.

## **Pages in this chapter**

| Page | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| [Helpdesk Dashboard](/hr/functional/v2.0/helpdesk/dashboard) | **Tickets → Dashboard** | Ticket volumes, resolution and SLA figures |
| [Tickets](/hr/functional/v2.0/helpdesk/tickets) | **Tickets → Tickets** | Create, assign and work tickets |
| [Helpdesk Configuration](/hr/functional/v2.0/helpdesk/configuration) | **Tickets → Configuration** | Department managers, ticket types and tags |

## **How the pieces fit**

1. **Ticket types** (for example *IT Service Desk, Benefits and Payroll*) are defined in [Configuration](/hr/functional/v2.0/helpdesk/configuration), each with an ID prefix such as *ITS* or *BEN*.
2. A [ticket](/hr/functional/v2.0/helpdesk/tickets) has an owner, a type, a priority, a deadline and a status, and is forwarded to a department, job position or individual.
3. Tickets move through the statuses **New, In Progress, On Hold, Resolved** and **Canceled**.
4. The [dashboard](/hr/functional/v2.0/helpdesk/dashboard) reports resolution rate, overdue tickets and SLA compliance.

::: info Scope
Pages are described as an administrator sees them. Creating tickets, changing a status, claiming, archiving and deleting change real records and were not exercised.
:::
