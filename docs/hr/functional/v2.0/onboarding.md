# **Onboarding**

The Onboarding module tracks shortlisted candidates and the tasks that get them ready to start. Open it from **Onboarding** in the sidebar. It follows [Recruitment](/hr/functional/v2.0/recruitment): candidates who are being hired appear here.

## **Pages in this chapter**

| Page | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| [Onboarding Dashboard](/hr/functional/v2.0/onboarding/dashboard) | **Onboarding → Dashboard** | Progress and completion figures |
| [Onboarding Candidates](/hr/functional/v2.0/onboarding/candidates) | **Onboarding → Candidates** | Shortlisted candidates, joining dates and portal invitations |
| [Onboarding Tasks](/hr/functional/v2.0/onboarding/tasks) | **Onboarding → Onboarding Tasks** | The onboarding pipeline of stages and tasks |

## **How the pieces fit**

1. A candidate shortlisted in Recruitment appears under [Candidates](/hr/functional/v2.0/onboarding/candidates), where a joining date and an offer-letter status are recorded and a portal invitation can be sent.
2. In [Onboarding Tasks](/hr/functional/v2.0/onboarding/tasks) the candidate moves through onboarding **stages**, each with tasks such as paperwork, orientation, equipment and training.
3. The [dashboard](/hr/functional/v2.0/onboarding/dashboard) totals how many are in progress and how many tasks are done.

::: info Scope
Pages are described as an administrator sees them. Sending portal invitations or mail, adding stages and changing statuses affect real people or send messages, and were not exercised. How a completed onboarding turns into an employee record is not described, because it was not observed.
:::
