# **Employee**

The Employee module holds the master record of everyone in the organisation: the employee list, each person's profile, the organisation chart, shift and work type requests, work schedules, company policies and disciplinary actions. Open it from **Employee** in the sidebar.

## **Pages in this chapter**

| Page | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| [My Dashboard](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/my-dashboard) | **Employee → My Dashboard** | Your own attendance, leave, payslips and goals |
| [Employees](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/employees) | **Employee → Employees** | The employee list, and adding an employee |
| [Employee Profile](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/employee-profile) | Click an employee, or **My Profile** | One person's record, in 18 tabs |
| [Organization Chart](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/organization-chart) | **Employee → Organization Chart** | Who reports to whom |

| [Work Schedules](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/work-schedules) | **Employee → Work Schedules** | Rotating shifts, rotating work types and the shift roster |
| [Policies & Discipline](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/policies-discipline) | **Employee → Policies & Discipline** | Company policies, FAQs and disciplinary actions |
| [Employee Configuration](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/configuration) | **Employee → Configuration** | Shifts, work types, employee types and tags |

## **Employee record and login account**

An employee record is the person's HR information. Signing in is a separate matter: the employee list offers **Send Invitation** and **Reset Password**, and a profile's **Groups & Permissions** tab shows the access that goes with the person.

## **Foundations to set up first**

New employees refer to shifts, work types, departments and job positions. The setup checklist on the [main dashboard](/hr/functional/v2.0/dashboard) lists the order Horilla suggests: **Company, Departments, Job Positions, Shift, Shift Schedule, Work Type, Mail Server, First Employee**. Shifts, shift schedules and work types are configured under [Employee Configuration](/hr/functional/v2.0/employee/configuration).

::: info Scope
Pages are described as an administrator sees them. What employees and managers see is not covered in this edition. Actions that change real records (saving, archiving, deleting, sending mail or invitations, resetting passwords) were not exercised on the demo.
:::
